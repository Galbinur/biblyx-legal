Frequently asked questions

Is Biblyx free?

Yes. Every reader gets a free daily allowance of AI-powered translations. Premium removes the daily limit with a monthly, six-month, or yearly plan.

What book formats can I import?

EPUB, PDF, and TXT. Your books stay on your device — Biblyx doesn't require uploading them anywhere.

Does Biblyx work offline?

Reading and highlighting always work offline. A built-in dictionary covers common words without an internet connection; new AI translations need one.

How does Biblyx track my vocabulary level?

Every word you save is automatically leveled from A1 to C2 (CEFR), and Word Lab shows your whole vocabulary broken down by level, so your progress is never a guess.

Do I need an account?

No. Biblyx works fully without an account. Sign in only if you want your vocabulary and progress backed up and synced across devices.

What languages does Biblyx support?

The Biblyx interface is available in 8 languages — English, Turkish, Azerbaijani, German, Spanish, French, Italian, and Russian — and you can read and translate books in the language you’re learning.

Is Biblyx available on iPhone?

Biblyx is available first on Android, via Google Play. iOS support is planned for a future release.

How is Biblyx different from a regular dictionary or flashcard app?

Biblyx builds your vocabulary from the real books you read, not generic word lists. Every saved word keeps the exact sentence you found it in, so flashcards use real context instead of a blank card.
