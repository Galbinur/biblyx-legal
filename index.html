How it works

1

Read

Import your own books, or start with one of 29 short stories and tales in 10 languages, and read in a calm reader with no dictionary tabs to switch to.

2

Tap

Tap any word to see its meaning in that sentence, or select a passage to translate it. Save the words you want to keep to WordLab.

3

Remember

Spaced-repetition flashcards bring saved words back after 1, 3, 7, 14 and 30 days, then at longer intervals.

Everything you need, nothing you don't

Your own EPUB, PDF and TXT books

Import EPUB, TXT and text-based PDF files, or open them with Biblyx from a file manager, browser or email. A table of contents takes you to any chapter.

Meaning that fits the sentence

The AI translates each word for the sentence you are reading and adds its part of speech, base form and an estimated CEFR level. Listen to it with your phone's text-to-speech voice.

Idioms and expressions

When a word is part of an idiom or expression, you see both meanings and can save either one as a card.

Grammar at a glance

See the article of German, Spanish, French, Italian and Portuguese nouns, and the parts of compound words.

Flashcards with real context

Every saved word keeps the sentence from your book, and you can add your own note to the card.

Review your way

Choose 10, 20 or 30 cards per review, undo an answer, or mark a card "I already know this". Words you keep forgetting are marked as struggling, and a quick multiple-choice test adds extra practice.

Export to Anki or CSV

Export your vocabulary as an .apkg file you can import into Anki, or as a CSV file for a spreadsheet. Only words are exported, never the text of your books.

Search, highlights and sync

Search inside a book, add bookmarks and highlight in four colors. Sign in to sync saved words, review progress, bookmarks and highlights between devices; book files stay on your device.

Premium

No daily limit on AI translations

Reading, WordLab, export and sync need no subscription, and there are no ads. AI translations of words and passages use a daily allowance. Premium removes the daily limit (fair use applies), with a monthly, six-month or yearly plan.

Frequently asked questions

Is Biblyx free?

Yes. Reading, WordLab, export, reminders and sync need no subscription, and there are no ads. AI translations of words and passages use a daily allowance. Premium removes the daily limit (fair use applies), with a monthly, six-month or yearly plan.

What book formats can I import?

EPUB, TXT and text-based PDF. Scanned PDFs, which are only images of pages, can't be read word by word. Your book files stay on your device and aren't uploaded to Biblyx's servers.

Does Biblyx work offline?

Yes, for reading and for reviewing your flashcards. Looking up a new word or translating a passage needs an internet connection.

How does Biblyx track my vocabulary level?

Every word you save gets an estimated CEFR level from A1 to C2, and WordLab shows your saved words by level.

Do I need an account?

No account is needed to read and translate. Sign in with email or Google to sync your saved words, review progress, bookmarks and highlights between devices, or to buy Premium.

Which languages can I learn?

Books in English, German, Spanish, French, Italian, Russian, Polish, Portuguese, Turkish and Azerbaijani can be translated; Swedish, Dutch, Ukrainian and Tajik are in beta. Books in other languages can be read, but not translated. The interface is available in the same 10 languages, and meanings appear in your interface language.

Can I export my words to Anki?

Yes. WordLab exports your words as an .apkg file you can import into Anki, with ready-made cards, or as a CSV file for Excel or Google Sheets. Only words are exported, never the text of your books.

Is Biblyx available on iPhone?

Not yet. Biblyx is available on Android through Google Play. An iOS version is planned, but there is no date yet.

How is Biblyx different from a dictionary or a flashcard app?

Biblyx builds your vocabulary from the books you read, not from ready-made word lists. Each meaning is fitted to the sentence you are reading, and each saved word keeps that sentence on its card.
