Biblyx — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

1. Introduction

Biblyx ("the app", "we", "us") is a reading and language-learning app made by Galbinur Guliyev, an individual developer based in Azerbaijan. Biblyx is not a company. This Privacy Policy explains what data the app processes and why, which service providers help us, how long data is kept, and what your rights are. It applies to all users, including users in the European Union.

2. Data Controller

The controller of your personal data is Galbinur Guliyev, the developer of Biblyx.
Contact: support@biblyx.com

Please send all privacy requests, including requests to use your rights in Section 8, to this address.

3. Data We Process

We process only the data the app needs to work:

  • Account: when you first open the app, an anonymous account (a random user ID) is created automatically, so the free daily limit works without registration. If you sign in with email, we store your email address; your password is kept only in protected (hashed) form. If you sign in with Google, we receive your email address and, from your Google profile, your name and profile picture link. Premium requires signing in.
  • Text you translate: when you tap a word, that word and the sentence it is in are sent for translation; when you select a passage, the passage is sent. When you import a book whose language the app cannot recognise on your device, up to 1,500 characters from its first pages and its title are sent once, only to detect the language. This text passes through our server function to OpenAI, which returns the result. Our server does not store it, and no account details (such as your email) are sent to OpenAI. Under its API terms, OpenAI does not use this data to train its models and may keep it for up to 30 days to detect abuse. Biblyx does not use it to train AI models either. If you are signed in, your translations are also saved to your account (see Cloud Sync).
  • Cloud Sync (only when you are signed in): your settings; WordLab — your saved words with their translations, the book sentence each word came from, your notes and your review progress; book details such as title, author, language and reading progress, plus a fingerprint (hash) of the file used to recognise the same book on your other devices — never the file itself; bookmarks; highlights, including the highlighted text; the translations you looked up; and a list of items you deleted, so the deletion also reaches your other devices.
  • Purchases: payments are made through Google Play; we never see your card details. RevenueCat, the service we use to manage subscriptions, receives your user ID and the purchase details from Google Play (product, dates and status) to confirm your subscription. On our server we store only your Premium plan and its expiry date.
  • Usage statistics (Firebase Analytics): counts and categories of how features are used (for example, that a translation was made), not linked to your account. Book titles, file contents, the words you look up and your notes are never sent to analytics. Firebase also collects standard technical data, such as device model, system version, approximate country and an ID for this app installation.
  • Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics): if the app crashes, technical error data (such as the error trace, device model and system version) is sent so we can fix the problem. It is not linked to your account.
  • App integrity check (Firebase App Check): the app asks Google Play to confirm that requests to our server come from the genuine Biblyx app, which blocks fake requests.
  • Abuse prevention: to stop the free daily limit from being bypassed (for example by reinstalling the app or switching accounts), our server keeps your device's Android ID with a daily translation count (so the count survives a reinstall); a hash of your phone's model details (the same for every phone of that model) with the date and your user ID; a daily translation count per account; and the times of your requests from the last day, to limit bursts. These records are used only for this purpose, never for advertising or profiling.
  • On your device: your books, library and saved words are stored on your device. If Android backup is turned on, Android may back up the app's data to your own Google account; sign-in data is excluded from this backup. That backup is managed by Google under your account, and we cannot access it.

4. Legal Bases (for users in the EU/EEA)

If you are in the European Economic Area, we rely on these legal bases:

  • Performance of a contract: to provide your account, translation, Cloud Sync and Premium.
  • Legitimate interests: to prevent abuse of the free limit, keep the service secure, understand which features are used and fix crashes. You can object to this at any time (Section 8).

5. Service Providers and International Transfers

We use the following service providers. Each processes data only to provide its service to us, under its own privacy policy:

  • Supabase — Database, sign-in, Cloud Sync, our server functions and account emails (such as sign-up confirmation and password reset). Our database is located in the EU (Frankfurt, Germany). supabase.com/privacy
  • OpenAI — AI translation and language detection for the text described in Section 3. openai.com/policies/privacy-policy
  • Google — Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics and App Check, Google Sign-In, Google Play billing, Android backup, and Google Cloud (BigQuery), where we analyse the usage statistics. policies.google.com/privacy
  • RevenueCat — Subscription management for Premium. revenuecat.com/privacy

Some of these providers process data outside the EU and Azerbaijan, mainly in the United States. Where the law requires it, these transfers rely on safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses. We do not sell your data, and the app shows no ads.

6. Your Books Stay on Your Device

Files you import (PDF, EPUB, TXT and other supported formats) are stored only on your device and are never uploaded — not to us and not to our providers. Only the pieces described in Section 3 leave your device: the text you translate, a one-time language sample, and, if you sign in, your Cloud Sync data. The rest of a book's content is never sent.

Biblyx is a software tool for reading content you import; it does not provide, distribute, license or check that content.

7. How Long We Keep Data

  • Account and Cloud Sync data: until you delete your account. Deletion is immediate and permanent. Copies may remain in our database provider's automatic backups for up to 30 days until they are overwritten; we never restore deleted accounts from them.
  • If you never sign in: our server holds only a random anonymous ID and the daily counts below; it holds no email and no synced content. Uninstalling the app deletes the data on your device.
  • Abuse-prevention records: 90 days; request times 1 day. When you delete your account, the records linked to your user ID are deleted at once. The Android ID counts are not linked to your account and are deleted after 90 days.
  • Premium status: deleted with your account. Google Play and RevenueCat keep their own purchase records under their own policies. Deleting your account or the app does not cancel a subscription; cancel it in Google Play.
  • Analytics and crash data: analytics data is kept for at most 14 months, including the copy in our Google Cloud project (BigQuery) used for analysis; crash reports are kept for 90 days.
  • OpenAI: up to 30 days on OpenAI's side, as described in Section 3.
  • On your device: until you delete it or uninstall the app.

8. Your Rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us to:

  • give you a copy of your data (access and portability);
  • correct inaccurate data;
  • delete your data. If you are signed in, you can do this yourself: open the menu, tap your email address, then "Delete my account". Anyone can also ask by email;
  • restrict processing, or object to processing based on legitimate interests.

Write to support@biblyx.com; we reply within one month. If you are in the EU/EEA, you can also complain to your national data protection authority.

9. Children's Privacy

Biblyx is not intended for children under 13, or under the higher minimum age that applies in your country (up to 16 in some EU countries), and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Sign-in data on your device is kept in encrypted storage, and on our server each account can reach only its own data. No system is 100% secure, but we work to protect your data.

11. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when it last changed.

12. Contact

For questions: support@biblyx.com

This policy is written to meet the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) for users in the EU/EEA, with the laws of the Republic of Azerbaijan in mind.
