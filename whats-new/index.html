What's new in Biblyx

By Galbinur Guliyev · Updated: 2026-11-09

Biblyx ships a new version every one to two weeks. This page lists what each version brought, in the words of its Google Play release notes, with the day it reached everyone. The current version is 1.3.0, in production since 7 November 2026. What the app is for doesn't change between versions: Learn languages by reading your own books.

Version 1.3.0, 7 November 2026

  • Paste any text (an article, a story, a message) and read it like a book
  • Your first book shows you which word to tap to see its meaning
  • A "Try again" button when a translation can't load
  • An optional daily reading reminder at a time you choose, offered once after your first read
  • Tips that show where your saved words go: flashcards in Word Lab

Version 1.2.1, 1 November 2026

  • Large TXT books open smoothly and no longer freeze the reader
  • Anki export: cleaner cards with just the Biblyx logo at the bottom
  • Reminders now work on devices that couldn't show the notification icon
  • Stability improvements and bug fixes

Version 1.2.0, 28 October 2026

  • Reading a book in your app's language? Choose which language its words are translated into
  • Word Lab: “Review expressions only” to practice just your saved expressions
  • Your reading position carries over to a second device or after reinstalling
  • Adding a book that's already in your library opens it instead of making a copy
  • Anki export in the app language, each word read with its own voice
  • More reliable sync and Google sign-in, plus bug fixes

Version 1.1.0, 21 October 2026

  • Smarter translations: when a word is part of an expression, you see both meanings
  • Table of contents for EPUB, TXT and PDF books
  • Flashcards: notes on cards, edit translations, “I already know this” and an undo arrow
  • Export your words to Anki (ready-made cards with pronunciation) or to CSV for Excel
  • Rename books; filter words and expressions separately
  • New beta book languages: Swedish, Dutch, Ukrainian, Tajik
  • Bug fixes and improvements

How updates arrive

Google Play installs updates by itself when auto-update is on; otherwise the Biblyx page on Google Play shows an Update button. The version on your phone is shown on the About screen inside the app. Updating never touches your saved words, flashcards, progress, bookmarks or highlights.

Frequently asked questions

How often is Biblyx updated?

Usually every one to two weeks; fixes go out faster. A version's notes appear here on the day it reaches Google Play.

Do I lose my saved words when I update?

No. Saved words, flashcards, review progress, bookmarks and highlights stay on your device, and with an account they are synced as well.

Where do I report a problem or ask for a feature?

Write to support@biblyx.com. Every message is read, and fixes usually go into the next version.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet. Biblyx is on Android through Google Play; an iOS version is planned, with no date.

Try it with your own book

Open an EPUB, PDF or TXT, or paste a text. Tap a word, see what it means in that sentence, save it and review it later.
