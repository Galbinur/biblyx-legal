Biblyx is a reader for people who learn a language from the books they already want to read. The idea in one line: **Learn languages by reading your own books**.

## Why reading works

Reading gives you the same words many times, each time in a sentence that shows what the word means. That is what researchers call *comprehensible input*: text you mostly understand, with a few new words per page.

- You see words in context, not in a list.
- You choose the book, so you stay interested.
- You read at your own speed, offline, anywhere.

## How to pick a book by level

1. Open the first page and count the words you don't know.
2. Fewer than two per page: too easy, pick something harder.
3. More than eight per page: too hard for now.
4. Between the two: start reading.

> A book you want to finish beats a book that is "good for you".

| Level | What to read | Unknown words per page |
|---|---|---|
| A2 | short stories, graded readers | 3–5 |
| B1 | young-adult novels, news | 3–6 |
| B2 | novels, essays | 2–5 |

## Frequently asked questions

### Does Biblyx work with PDF files?

Yes, with PDFs that contain text. Scanned PDFs are pictures of pages, so there is nothing to tap.

### Do I need an account?

No. Reading and translating work without one; an account syncs your words between devices.
